# verify-catalogs
- **partly-true**: 1. Size ranking: Audius ~1M+ > Jamendo 644,709 > Pixabay 290,000+ > FMA 168,248 > IA Netlabels 77,072 releases > ccMixter 51,803 > YouTube Audio Library; Bandcamp/SoundCloud bigger but not free/open.
  - CORRECTION: The Jamendo, Pixabay, FMA, IA Netlabels and ccMixter numbers all check out live today. Problems: (a) The Audius '1M+ tracks / 250k artists' figure is Audius's own number from June 2022 (reported by Axios). It is not a 2025 figure, and no current official count exists. (b) The ranking is inconsistent. Audius is counted for being free to stream, but on that test SoundCloud and Audiomack would also count and are far larger. On the open-licence test, Audius is mostly not CC. (c) Limiting Internet Archive to the netlabels collection undercounts it badly. IA has 1,964,096 audio items with a Creative Commons licence URL, and 168,798 of those have electronic-type subjects. These are items, not tracks, and include podcasts and spoken word, but IA is still arguably the biggest CC audio pool. (d) Openverse also lists Wikimedia Commons audio (3,982,160) and Freesound (591,898). Neither is a song catalogue, but they are bigger by raw count. (e) ccMixter's 51,803 counts uploads, including a cappellas and samples.
- **partly-true**: 2. Usefulness ranking for TwinSpin: Audius > IA Netlabels > Jamendo > Openverse > FMA > Pixabay > ccMixter > Bandcamp/YTAL/SoundCloud.
  - CORRECTION: The order still holds up, but Audius's lead is smaller than claimed. Audius's own API Terms (updated 2 July 2025) allow only session-based caching and ban scraping or data harvesting. That makes it a streaming-only source for TwinSpin's IndexedDB library, just like Jamendo. Openverse is weaker than implied. Anonymous use is capped at 20 requests/min and 200/day, page_size of at most 20, and 240 results (12 pages) per query; page 13 returns 401. IA should cover all CC-licensed audio, not just collection:netlabels, which is about 5x more electronic material. Its storage nodes were not fully reliable: 2 of 5 timed out from this machine.
- **confirmed**: 3. Audius works end to end from a static origin with CORS * on every hop, a 300 s signed URL, no key needed for reads, and a Free plan of 10 req/s and 500k req/month.
  - CORRECTION: Confirmed live. api.audius.co returns 200 with ACAO *. /v1/tracks/{id}/stream returns a 302 with ACAO * to creatornode.audius.co. That node returns a 307 with ACAO * to R2 storage. R2 returns 206 audio/mpeg with ACAO * and Content-Range. The MP3 frame header is MPEG-1 Layer III at 320 kbps / 48 kHz. Small fixes: X-Amz-Expires was 300 s on 3 of 4 tracks and 436 s on 1, so treat it as about 5 to 7 minutes and never store the URL. Keyless reads work even without app_name. The 10 req/s and 500k/month figures are the documented Free plan for an API key; keyless limits are not published. Any use of the API binds you to the Audius API Terms.
- **confirmed**: 4. Audius track JSON includes bpm and musical_key (72 of 100 trending Techno tracks have both), and 10 electronic genre filters each return 100 trending tracks.
  - CORRECTION: Reproduced exactly: 72 of 100 have both, and every track with a BPM also has a key. All 10 genres (and Ambient) returned 100. Caveats: is_custom_bpm was false on all 100, so these values are auto-detected and sometimes wrong. A Schranz remix was reported at 100 BPM. TwinSpin should still run its own BPM detection. A 'Minimal' genre query returned only 2 tracks.
- **partly-true**: 5. Audius content is not CC (11 'All rights reserved', 1 CC BY-NC-SA, rest unlicensed, 13 downloadable). Persistent IndexedDB caching is not explicitly permitted because no API terms were found.
  - CORRECTION: The licence and download counts are correct. The terms part is refuted. Audius does publish API Terms (Tiki Labs Inc. d/b/a Audius, last updated 2 July 2025) at https://audius.co/documents/ApiTerms.pdf, linked from audius.co/legal/api-terms. They explicitly allow only session-based caching, and cached content must become unavailable when the session ends. So persistent IndexedDB caching is explicitly prohibited, not just unpermitted. The terms also ban scraping and data harvesting, require apps to give users the Audius Privacy Policy and API Terms, and let Audius terminate at any time. They make no exception for is_downloadable tracks. Also, 7 of the 13 'downloadable' tracks are follow-gated (download_conditions.follow_user_id), so only 6 of 100 could be downloaded without signing in.
- **confirmed**: 6. Jamendo API terms: non-commercial only (ads and affiliate revenue count as commercial), no apps designed to cache or offer offline access, attribution plus a link to each track required, 35,000 req/month, limit max 200.
  - CORRECTION: The clauses match the official terms verbatim. Note the terms were last modified on 17 Sept 2013. Attribution must also credit JAMENDO as the provider, not just the artist, and each item needs a backlink. Max 200 per call is confirmed in the docs. The 35,000 requests/month figure appears only in third-party sources and the devportal plan shown after signup. It is not on any public official page, so treat it as unverified.
- **confirmed**: 7. Jamendo CORS: api.jamendo.com ACAO *, prod-1.storage reflects the Origin with 206 Range, mp3l.jamendo.com ACAO *. Electronic is the largest genre in MTG-Jamendo (16,480 of ~55.5k).
  - CORRECTION: Confirmed, with nuances. api.jamendo.com sends ACAO * only when a client_id parameter is present. Without one, the response (HTTP 200 with an error body) has no ACAO. An OPTIONS preflight to prod-1.storage returns 403, so only simple GETs with no custom headers will work. mp3l is the 96 kbps mp31 format; DJ-quality mp32 (VBR) or FLAC comes from prod-1.storage. Jamendo has no BPM field, only a coarse 'speed' enum. MTG-Jamendo is 55,701 tracks, and electronic is first with 16,480 tracks and 1,546 artists.
- **partly-true**: 8. IA Netlabels: 77,072 releases, 34,791 electronic-type, licence split (31,163 BY-NC-ND; 14,705 BY-NC-SA; 4,360 BY-ND; 2,959 BY-SA; 2,330 PD/CC0 of 61,230), 1,068 added in 2025, LMA 305,327 with 2,091 electronic, CORS * throughout.
  - CORRECTION: Most numbers match: 77,072; 61,230; 31,163; 14,705; 2,959; 2,330; 1,068; 305,327; techno 5,953; house 2,819. My electronic-type query gave 34,817, which is close. Error: the '4,360 BY-ND' figure includes 1,159 CC 1.0 'by-nd-nc' items, which are NonCommercial. True BY-ND, where commercial use is OK, is 3,201, and the NC-ND total is about 32,322. The 2,330 PD figure includes 1,071 CC0. 758 more releases have been added in 2026 so far. The LMA electronic count depends on the query: 253 for subject:electronic, 2,350 for broader subjects. CORS: advancedsearch, metadata and the /download 302 all send ACAO *. Storage nodes send ACAO * when reachable, but 2 of 5 timed out.
- **confirmed**: 9. FMA is owned by Tribe of Noise (since 2019, © 2026) with 168,248 tracks (43,027 Electronic) and no public API; only files.freemusicarchive.org carries CORS *.
  - CORRECTION: All confirmed. Detail: the page's playbackUrl (freemusicarchive.org/track/<slug>/stream/) is a 302 without ACAO, and a CORS fetch fails on a redirect without ACAO. Only the direct files.freemusicarchive.org/storage-freemusicarchive-org/tracks/*.mp3 URL, which has to be scraped, works (206 with ACAO *). Techno has 3,007 tracks and House 2,073. The acquisition was announced in Sept 2019.
- **confirmed**: 10. ccMixter cannot be used from a browser: the API has no CORS and audio returns 403 unless the Referer is ccmixter.org.
  - CORRECTION: Confirmed. The API returns Content-Type text/plain with no ACAO. Audio returns 403 with no Referer or a github.io Referer, and 206 only with a ccmixter.org Referer, which a browser page cannot spoof. Even that 206 response has no ACAO. Side note: the ccMixter API exposes a bpm field.
- **confirmed**: 11. The Pixabay API has no music endpoint, and its licence allows modification and commercial use without attribution but forbids standalone redistribution.
  - CORRECTION: The API docs list only /api/ (images) and /api/videos/; /api/audio/ returns 400. The licence summary matches. Note that pixabay.com blocks curl (403 from Cloudflare), so scraping is not viable either.
- **confirmed**: 12. SoundCloud: self-serve credentials need Artist Pro (18 May 2026), client credentials need the secret so a backend is required, and the terms ban file-save, caching, offline use and modification without consent.
  - CORRECTION: Confirmed. The guide also says 'All clients are currently treated as confidential rather than public', so even the PKCE code exchange needs client_secret. The terms also require backlinks and crediting SoundCloud, and ban ads around User Content. Terms effective 30 March 2024.
- **confirmed**: 13. CC licences and DJing (BY/BY-SA/CC0 fine; NC risky for paid gigs; ND bars sharing adaptations; synching music to video is always an adaptation; CC does not replace venue PRO licences).
  - CORRECTION: Substantively right. Exact NC text: 'not primarily intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation'. The synch rule is in the 4.0 legal code itself (Section 1(a)), not just a CC policy. CC 'Share' includes public performance. The licensor waives royalties only where waivable, and reserves non-waivable collecting-society royalties and royalties for non-NC use. So venue blanket PRO licences can still apply. CC has no official FAQ ruling on live DJ mixing of ND works; it remains a grey area.
- **confirmed**: 14. Bandcamp has no public catalogue API (label/merch APIs only), and purchases download as MP3 V0/320, FLAC, AAC, Ogg, ALAC, WAV and AIFF.
  - CORRECTION: Confirmed. The APIs (Account, Sales Report, Merch Orders) are for 'Labels and merchandise fulfillment partners'. Access is granted by emailing Bandcamp, then uses OAuth2 with a client secret. Lossless formats exist only if the artist uploaded lossless; ALAC is capped at 48 kHz.
## Extra findings
- VERDICT/RECOMMENDATION error: The plan to 'keep tracks session-only unless is_downloadable is true' goes against the Audius API Terms (https://audius.co/documents/ApiTerms.pdf, 2 July 2025). The terms allow only session-based caching and make no exception for downloadable tracks. Safer: Audius in TwinSpin is stream-only. For a permanent copy, the user downloads the file in the Audius app, where the artist allows it, then imports it through file, Drive or NAS. 7 of 13 'downloadable' trending Techno tracks were follow-gated.
- Audius API Terms also require giving app users the Audius Privacy Policy and the API Terms, ban scraping/data harvesting and AI training, and let Audius terminate access at any time without notice. All of this belongs in the 'catch' paragraph, not 'no published API terms found'.
- Audius BPM and key are auto-detected (is_custom_bpm=false on 100/100), and some are clearly wrong (a Schranz remix tagged 100 BPM). Show them as hints and keep TwinSpin's own BPM analysis.
- The '1M+ tracks / 250k+ artists' Audius figure is Audius's own number from June 2022 (Axios), not a 2025 measurement. Present it as 'Audius said 1M+ tracks in 2022; no current official count'. Audius's docs do call it 'the largest open music catalog on the internet' (https://docs.audius.co/llms.txt), which is self-description.
- Missed CC source size: Internet Archive has 1,964,096 audio items with a Creative Commons licence URL. 168,798 of them carry electronic/techno/house/ambient/idm/minimal/trance/edm subjects, about 5x the netlabels-only 34.8k. A TwinSpin IA integration should query all CC-licensed audio (licenseurl:*creativecommons*) rather than just collection:netlabels, and filter licences per item. Quality and curation vary.
- IA licence filter: the 1,159 netlabels items under CC 1.0 'by-nd-nc' are NonCommercial. A naive '*by-nd*' match would wrongly treat them as commercial-OK.
- Openverse anonymous limits (from live headers): 20 req/min burst, 200 req/day sustained, page_size of at most 20, and at most 240 results per query (page 13 returns 401). Its ToS forbid scraping. Fine as a search front end for one user, not for browsing a whole catalogue.
- Jamendo's API only sends ACAO * when a client_id query parameter is present, and prod-1.storage rejects preflights (403). TwinSpin must fetch Jamendo audio with a plain GET and no custom headers; the existing fetch()->ArrayBuffer flow does this. The default 'audio' field is the 96 kbps mp31; request audioformat=mp32 or flac for DJ use.
- FMA's own /track/<slug>/stream/ redirect lacks ACAO, so a CORS fetch fails at the redirect. Only scraped direct files.freemusicarchive.org URLs work. This reinforces 'no integration' for FMA.
- Two Internet Archive storage nodes (dn601703.us.archive.org, dn601708.us.archive.org) timed out from this network while others worked. IA integration needs retries/timeouts and a clear error state.
- For a psychedelic-leaning user, a niche option is Ektoplazm (free psytrance netlabel portal; 82 IA hits). It is small and not the biggest, but could be mentioned as a manual-download source. Not verified further.
