# research-spotify
## Verdict
Partly possible. TwinSpin can have a separate, optional "Spotify" player. The listener signs in with their own Premium account using Authorization Code with PKCE, which works on a static site with no server and no client secret. The Web Playback SDK then turns the TwinSpin tab into a Spotify Connect device called "TwinSpin". From there the page can play, pause, skip, seek, set volume, shuffle, repeat and add tracks to the queue through the Web API. The page can also search, and it can list the user's own playlists. TwinSpin cannot put Spotify audio into its DJ engine, so Spotify tracks get no decoding, BPM or beat maps, scratching, EQ, FX, crossfading or two-deck mixing. There are two separate reasons for this. Technically, the SDK plays Widevine/EME-encrypted streams, and browsers keep decrypted EME media away from Web Audio and canvas. The un-encrypted 30-second preview URLs and the audio-features and audio-analysis endpoints have also been closed to new apps since 2024-11-27. Legally, Developer Policy III.7 says "Do not permit any device or system to segue, mix, re-mix, or overlap any Spotify Content". Developer Terms IV.2 forbid modifying content, "stream ripping" and caching. Spotify did bring DJ mixing back on 2025-09-24, but only through licensed partner deals: rekordbox, Serato and djay first, then mobile on 2025-12-11, then VirtualDJ, Cross DJ and edjing on 2026-09-15. There is no public program to apply to, and that use is still personal only, with no clubs or livestreams. In practice the app also stays in Development Mode for good. Since 2026-02-11 for new apps (2026-03-09 for existing ones), that means the app owner needs Premium and at most 5 allowlisted users can use it. Extended quota has been open only to organisations with at least 250k MAU since 2025-05-15.
## Recommendation
Do not try to put Spotify into the DJ decks; that route is closed both technically (EME/DRM) and by the terms (Policy III.7, Terms IV.2). Offer an optional, clearly separate "Spotify" mode on the music-player page, built as option A with option B as fallback. (1) PKCE login with no backend. Register https://ranama.github.io/twinspin/ and http://127.0.0.1:PORT/ as redirect URIs. Store tokens in localStorage, and on invalid_grant (6-month expiry) send the user through login again. (2) The Web Playback SDK registers a device called "TwinSpin" and transfers playback to it with PUT /me/player. Call activateElement() from the user's tap, and handle autoplay_failed and account_error (Premium needed). If the SDK fails on a phone (Widevine errors), fall back to remote-controlling the user's Spotify app through the Web API. (3) Features: search (10 per page), the user's own playlists and library, play/pause, next/previous, seek, volume, shuffle, repeat, add to queue, and now-playing with cover art, metadata, a 'Listen on Spotify' link and Spotify attribution. (4) Hard rules: Spotify and the TwinSpin decks never play at the same time (starting one pauses the other), and none of TwinSpin's transitions, crossfades, EQ, FX, BPM, waveform or scratching ever apply to Spotify. Do not cache or record Spotify audio, and treat it as personal, non-commercial use. (5) Tell users clearly: the app owner needs Premium; each listener needs a full (not mobile-only) Premium plan; at most 5 accounts can use it, added by hand to the allowlist in the developer dashboard; it can never be opened to the public because extended quota is for organisations with at least 250k MAU. Keep the mode minimal so it does not 'replicate a core user experience of Spotify' (III.11). For anyone who wants to actually DJ with their Spotify library, point them to the licensed partner apps: rekordbox, djay, Serato, VirtualDJ, Cross DJ, edjing. Effort is about 1–2 days, mostly testing on iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
## Options
### A. Spotify player tab using the Web Playback SDK and Web API (in-browser playback)
- **whatWorks**: TwinSpin becomes a Spotify Connect device in the browser. Controls come from the SDK and the Web API player endpoints: play, pause or togglePlay, next and previous track, seek, volume, transfer playback (PUT /me/player), shuffle, repeat (off/track/context) and add to queue (POST /me/player/queue). It can read the queue and now-playing state (artwork, title, artist, position). The Web API adds search (since Feb 2026 at most 10 results per page, 5 by default, use offset for more), the user's own or collaborative playlists and their items (/playlists/{id}/items), saved library (/me/library), and single-item track/album/artist lookups (batch lookups were removed). It cannot touch the audio at all: no BPM, waveform, beat grid, scratching, EQ, FX, pitch or tempo change, and no crossfades or TwinSpin transitions between a Spotify track and anything else. Spotify audio must never overlap TwinSpin deck audio. There is no endpoint to remove or reorder queue items, only to add (medium confidence). Recommendations, related artists, featured or category playlists and Spotify editorial or algorithmic playlists are not available to a new app.
- **requirements**: Each listener needs Spotify Premium. The SDK fires account_error otherwise, and the docs say 'mobile only' Premium plans do not qualify. Someone must register a developer app at developer.spotify.com. It stays in Development Mode, and since 2026-02-11 (new apps) and 2026-03-09 (existing apps) the owner needs active Premium or the app stops working. At most 5 users, each added by hand to the allowlist. Request quota is counted per developer account (July 2026), and going over returns 429 QUOTA_EXCEEDED. Up to 25 client IDs per developer (raised from 1 in July 2026). Extended quota is not realistic: since 2025-05-15 it is for organisations only, needs at least 250k MAU and a launched commercial service, and review takes up to 6 weeks. Refresh tokens expire 6 months after the user first authorised (new apps since 2026-06-18, existing apps since 2026-07-20), so the user must sign in again then. Scopes: streaming (the SDK), user-read-playback-state, user-modify-playback-state, user-read-currently-playing, and playlist-read-private / user-library-read if playlists or the library are shown. No backend is needed.
- **termsAndLegal**: Allowed: personal, non-commercial streaming to Premium users, on condition that the page shows cover art and metadata during playback (Policy II.5), credits Spotify, and links back to the content on Spotify (II.4). Forbidden: 'segue, mix, re-mix, or overlap any Spotify Content' (Policy III.7). That covers crossfades and transitions, and playing a Spotify track at the same time as a local deck. Also forbidden: synchronising recordings with visual media (III.6), modifying or creating derivative works (Terms IV.2(a)(ii)), 'stream ripping' (IV.2(b)(iii)), caching beyond what is strictly necessary (IV.3), training AI/ML (Policy III.14), and broadcast or public play from a business or targeting businesses (III.10). Policy IV.2 also says commercial uses are not permitted for SDAs. Risk: III.11 says not to build products that 'mimic, or replicate or attempt to replace a core user experience of Spotify'. A full 'listen to Spotify in TwinSpin instead of their app' player sits close to that line. A minimal personal-use add-on in dev mode is low-risk but not explicitly blessed. Both the Policy and the Terms (v10) took effect 2025-05-15.
- **technical**: Auth: Authorization Code with PKCE runs entirely in the browser. The client ID is public, there is no secret, and the token exchange is a direct fetch to accounts.spotify.com/api/token, as in Spotify's own SPA tutorial. The implicit grant, plain-HTTP redirects and 'localhost' redirect URIs were removed for new apps on 2025-04-09 and for all apps on 2025-11-27. Register https://ranama.github.io/twinspin/ (HTTPS is fine) and, for local testing, http://127.0.0.1:PORT/ (not localhost; you can register it without a port and add the port at request time). Store tokens in localStorage, and on 'invalid_grant' send the user through sign-in again. SDK: load https://sdk.scdn.co/spotify-player.js and create new Spotify.Player({name:'TwinSpin', getOAuthToken, volume, enableMediaSession}). On 'ready' you get a device_id, then call PUT /me/player to transfer playback to it. The page must be a secure context, and any iframe needs allow='encrypted-media; autoplay'. Audio is Widevine/EME-decrypted inside the browser's media pipeline, which the spec and browsers keep away from Web Audio. A MediaElementAudioSourceNode would get silence or nothing at all, and no SDK method returns audio data. Browsers: Spotify says the SDK works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge on desktop, Android and iOS, and mobile support has been official since 2021-09-13. Known mobile caveats: autoplay is blocked until activateElement() runs from a user tap (watch for the 'autoplay_failed' event), background play is limited, and on iOS setVolume does nothing because volume follows the hardware buttons. Some Android Chrome devices fail with Widevine errors such as 'No supported keysystem', 'Unable to set L1 security level' or 'Device not provisioned'. Player API calls are not guaranteed to run in order. The Web API is CORS-enabled for browser calls.
- **effort**: Medium, about 1–2 days. Covers the PKCE login and refresh module, the SDK device with a ready/transfer handshake, the now-playing UI with art, link-back and attribution, search and playlist browsing, queue/shuffle/repeat controls through the Web API, and handling for mobile autoplay, Premium (account_error) and token expiry. Then test on iOS Safari and Android Chrome. Also needed: a hard rule that starting Spotify pauses both TwinSpin decks and starting a deck pauses Spotify, plus no transitions applied to Spotify.
- sources: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-playback-sdk https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-playback-sdk/reference https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2021-09-13-web-playback-sdk-out-of-beta https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-playback-sdk/tutorials/getting-started https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/reference/add-to-queue https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/scopes https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/tutorials/code-pkce-flow https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/redirect_uri https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2025-02-12-increasing-the-security-requirements-for-integrating-with-spotify https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2025-10-14-reminder-oauth-migration-27-nov-2025 https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/tutorials/february-2026-migration-guide https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/quota-modes https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/references/changes/july-2026 https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2026-06-18-refresh-token-expiration https://developer.spotify.com/policy https://developer.spotify.com/terms https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/Web-Playback-SDK-Not-Working-on-Latest-Chrome-Android/m-p/5632888/highlight/true
### B. Remote control only (Web API controls the user's native Spotify app over Spotify Connect, no SDK)
- **whatWorks**: TwinSpin acts as a remote. It lists the user's devices (GET /me/player/devices) and controls whichever one is active, such as the Spotify app on the same phone, a desktop or a speaker. Controls are play, pause, next, previous, seek, volume (where the device allows), shuffle, repeat, add to queue, and starting a playlist, album or track. Search, playlists and now-playing display work as in option A. Audio comes out of Spotify's own app, so the mobile EME, autoplay and background-play problems of the SDK go away. No audio access and no DJ processing.
- **requirements**: Same as option A: listener Premium for the player control endpoints, a dev-mode app with an owner who has Premium, 5 allowlisted users, PKCE, and re-authentication every 6 months. The Spotify app must be open and active on some device; a sleeping or killed iOS app can drop off the device list (medium confidence).
- **termsAndLegal**: Lowest legal risk of the playback options. TwinSpin only sends commands and Spotify's own client does the playback. Still applies: attribution, cover art and metadata with a link back, personal non-commercial use, no overlapping with TwinSpin deck audio (III.7), and the III.11 'replicate core experience' caution.
- **technical**: Plain fetch calls to api.spotify.com with a Bearer token from a static page. No SDK script and no Widevine. Works the same on GitHub Pages and on 127.0.0.1. Player calls are not guaranteed to run in order, so poll GET /me/player or GET /me/player/queue to refresh the UI.
- **effort**: Low to medium, about 0.5–1 day. It reuses the PKCE module from option A. A good fallback for phones where the SDK is unreliable.
- sources: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/reference/add-to-queue https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/scopes https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/tutorials/code-pkce-flow https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/quota-modes https://developer.spotify.com/policy
### C. Spotify Embed iFrame API (no developer app)
- **whatWorks**: An embedded Spotify widget for a track, album or playlist that TwinSpin's JavaScript can control: loadUri/loadEntity, play, pause, resume, togglePlay, seek, restart, plus playback_update events (position, duration, isPaused). Full tracks play only when the listener is logged in to spotify.com in the same browser and the third-party Spotify cookies are allowed. Otherwise it plays 30-second previews, and mobile and WebView reports are mixed. There is no API for queue, shuffle or repeat, no access to the user's library, and no audio access.
- **requirements**: No developer app, no client ID and no OAuth. Full tracks still depend on the listener's own Spotify login session in the browser. Widget Terms of Use apply.
- **termsAndLegal**: The Widget Terms allow embedding Spotify's player as provided. The embed places Spotify and third-party cookies, so a consent notice may be needed. The audio cannot be mixed or overlapped with TwinSpin output.
- **technical**: Spotify's iframe, with audio sandboxed inside it, so nothing is reachable from Web Audio. Safari blocks autoplay without a user gesture. Third-party-cookie blocking (Safari ITP, Firefox) often limits it to previews.
- **effort**: Low, a few hours. Value is limited: unreliable full playback and no real queue, shuffle or repeat.
- sources: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/embeds/references/iframe-api https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/embeds/terms https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/30-second-preview-showing-when-try-to-embed-playlist/m-p/5655484/highlight/true
### D. Spotify tracks loaded into TwinSpin's DJ decks (decodeAudioData, BPM, scratching, EQ, FX, crossfade, two-deck mix)
- **whatWorks**: Nothing. There is no way to get PCM: SDK audio is DRM/EME-protected, the 30-second preview_url was removed for new apps on 2024-11-27, and capturing the audio would be stream ripping. Track analysis is also unavailable, because audio-features and audio-analysis (the old BPM, key and beat data) return 403 for apps created on or after 2024-11-27, so even a metadata-only BPM display would not work.
- **requirements**: Would need a licensed partner deal like the ones rekordbox, Serato, djay, VirtualDJ, Cross DJ and edjing have. Spotify publishes no open program or application path.
- **termsAndLegal**: Prohibited for a normal developer app. Policy III.7 bars segue, mix, re-mix and overlap. Terms IV.2(a)(ii) bar 'modifying, editing, altering, creating derivative works'. IV.2(b)(iii) bars stream ripping, and IV.3 bars caching. Even the official partner DJ integrations are limited to personal, non-commercial use, with public performance in 'clubs, venues, events, or livestreams' not allowed.
- **technical**: EME-decrypted media is deliberately kept away from Web Audio, so a MediaElementAudioSourceNode on a protected element gets no samples. The audio-features and audio-analysis endpoints return 403 to new apps, and the analysis APIs are deprecated.
- **effort**: Not feasible.
- sources: https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2024-11-27-changes-to-the-web-api https://developer.spotify.com/policy https://developer.spotify.com/terms https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1071482 https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-audio/2014JanMar/0031.html
### E. Become an official Spotify DJ software partner
- **whatWorks**: This is what rekordbox, Serato and djay got on 2025-09-24, mobile rekordbox and djay on 2025-12-11, and VirtualDJ, Cross DJ and edjing on 2026-09-15. It covers mixing the full catalogue and playlists with up to 4 tracks at once, online only. Stems, offline caching, recording and USB export are reported as unavailable in rekordbox. Some albums, artist pages and other content are not available in DJ software.
- **requirements**: A business-level licensing and technical agreement with Spotify and the rights holders. Spotify publishes no application route. One third-party DJ company reported being rejected for extended access. Extended quota needs an organisation with at least 250k MAU.
- **termsAndLegal**: Partner use is still personal and non-commercial, and public performance (clubs, venues, events, livestreams) is not permitted under Spotify's support terms.
- **technical**: Uses proprietary partner integrations, not the public Web API or Web Playback SDK.
- **effort**: Not achievable for TwinSpin, a personal static site. Users who want to DJ with Spotify can use one of the partner apps.
- sources: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-09-24/dj-software-integration-premium/ https://support.spotify.com/us/article/dj-integration/ https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/24/spotify-now-integrates-directly-with-dj-software-from-rekordbox-serato-and-djay https://djtechtools.com/2025/09/24/spotify-finally-slides-into-rekordbox-serato-and-algoriddim-djay-again-for-better-or-worse/ https://www.ballparkdj.com/spotify https://musictech.com/news/gear/spotify-stopping-3rd-party-dj-app-support/
## Findings
- [high,critical] Spotify audio cannot be routed into TwinSpin's Web Audio DJ engine. The Web Playback SDK plays Widevine/EME-protected streams, browsers keep decrypted EME media away from Web Audio and canvas, and no SDK method returns audio data. Beatmatching, scratching, EQ and FX on Spotify tracks are technically impossible in the browser.
- [high,critical] Developer Policy III.7 (effective 2025-05-15) says 'Do not permit any device or system to segue, mix, re-mix, or overlap any Spotify Content'. Developer Terms v10 IV.2 forbid modifying, editing, altering or creating derivative works, and facilitating 'stream ripping'. IV.3 forbids local caching beyond what is strictly necessary. Crossfades, transitions and overlapping a Spotify track with a TwinSpin deck are therefore not allowed.
- [high,critical] Basic playback is allowed and doable. The Web Playback SDK makes 'a local Spotify Connect device in your browser'. Web API player endpoints cover transfer, play, pause, skip, seek, volume, shuffle, repeat and add-to-queue (POST /me/player/queue, Premium only, scope user-modify-playback-state). The SDK needs the 'streaming' scope and a Premium account; mobile-only Premium plans do not qualify.
- [high,critical] Development Mode changed on 2026-02-11 for new apps and 2026-03-09 for existing ones. The app owner needs active Premium or the app stops working, each app is capped at 5 allowlisted users (down from 25), and search limit fell to 10 (default 5). Batch GETs, browse, artist top-tracks and other users' playlists or profiles were removed, and playlist items are returned only for playlists the user owns or collaborates on. Player endpoints were not changed.
- [high,critical] Since 2025-05-15, extended quota mode accepts only organisations (not individuals) with a launched service and at least 250k MAU, and review takes up to 6 weeks. TwinSpin will stay in Development Mode with at most 5 users.
- [high,critical] A static site with no server can use Authorization Code with PKCE: no client secret, and the token exchange is a direct browser fetch. Implicit grant, HTTP redirect URIs and 'localhost' redirect URIs were dropped for new apps from 2025-04-09 and for all apps on 2025-11-27. Register https://ranama.github.io/twinspin/ and http://127.0.0.1:PORT (loopback may skip the port).
- [high] Refresh tokens now expire 6 months after the user first authorised, and refreshing does not reset the clock. New apps have been affected since 2026-06-18 and existing apps since 2026-07-20. On invalid_grant, send the user through PKCE login again.
- [high] In July 2026 client IDs per developer went from 1 to 25, dev-mode quota is now counted per developer account, and going over returns 429 with reason QUOTA_EXCEEDED.
- [high,critical] On 2024-11-27 Spotify closed these to new apps: audio-features, audio-analysis, recommendations, related-artists, featured and category playlists, 30-second preview URLs in multi-get responses, and algorithmic or editorial playlists. TwinSpin cannot get Spotify BPM or beat data or any un-encrypted preview MP3.
- [medium] Mobile browsers: Spotify says the SDK works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge on Android and iOS (mobile support since 2021-09-13). Caveats: autoplay is blocked until activateElement() runs from a user tap ('autoplay_failed' event), background play is limited, iOS ignores setVolume, and some Android devices fail Widevine provisioning (L1/L3, 'No supported keysystem').
- [medium] Policy III.11 says not to build products that 'mimic, or replicate or attempt to replace a core user experience of Spotify'. A full 'use TwinSpin instead of the Spotify app' player is a grey area. Keep the Spotify tab minimal, credit Spotify, show cover art and metadata (II.5), and link back (II.4).
- [high,critical] History: Spotify left all third-party DJ apps on 2020-07-01. It came back on 2025-09-24 through partner integrations (rekordbox, Serato, djay; Premium; 51 markets). Mobile rekordbox and djay followed on 2025-12-11, and VirtualDJ, Cross DJ and edjing on 2026-09-15. These use 'licensing and technical agreements', not the public API. They allow up to 4 tracks mixed at once, online only, with no stems or offline mode reported. Use is personal only, with no clubs, venues, events or livestreams. No open partner application exists.
- [high] Policy III.10: Spotify is for personal, non-commercial use and must not be played publicly from a business. Policy IV.2: 'commercial uses are not permitted for SDAs'. Policy IV.1: streaming is only for subscribers.
- [medium] The Spotify Embed iFrame API needs no developer app (loadUri/loadEntity, play, pause, resume, seek, playback_update). It plays full tracks only for users logged in to spotify.com in the same browser, with Spotify's third-party cookies allowed; otherwise it plays 30-second previews. It has no queue, shuffle or repeat API.
