# research-suno
## Verdict
TwinSpin cannot legitimately log in to a user's Suno account and stream their library into the decks. Suno has no public API. In July 2026 it started looking into a curated developer API built around generation, and nothing has shipped: no docs, no pricing, no date. The only cookie-based wrappers available need a server and CAPTCHA solving, and they break Suno's terms. A plain "paste a Suno link" loader no longer works either. On 2026-10-13 I tested a public v6 song (f0f00357-0cd7-4fd5-96e5-d9314439f4e5, 240 s). The page's audio_url field now points to studio-api.prod.suno.com/api/forbidden. The old cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp3 returns 403. The stream Suno's own player uses is listed as "m4a-opus" on d2lwuy8qc234o3.cloudfront.net. That host does send Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *, but the bytes look scrambled: no ftyp/moov/mdat boxes and full-entropy data, so decodeAudioData cannot read them. The share video at cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp4 (H.264 + AAC) is public, sends ACAO *, and decodes in practice. But fetching it to get the audio skips the paid download allowance and falls under the Terms' bans on stream ripping, scraping, and access "through any means not intentionally made available". CORS is not what blocks this, so a proxy would not help. The terms and the encryption are the blockers. What TwinSpin can do, fully and within the rules: the user downloads their own song from Suno, then loads it with Add music. Every Suno plan offers MP3, and Pro/Premier also offer WAV. Since 2026-09-03 downloads are capped: Free gets up to 7 lifetime trial downloads for non-commercial use only, Pro gets 20 a month, Premier 60 a month, and Premier inside Suno Studio is unlimited. A downloaded file runs through the full DJ engine: BPM and beat map, scratching, EQ/FX, mixing, queue, shuffle. Suno's official embed player (suno.com/embed/<id>, framing allowed) could optionally be shown on the music-player page. It only plays inside Suno's iframe and cannot be routed into the Web Audio engine.
## Recommendation
Don't build a Suno account connection or a 'paste Suno link' loader. Build the compliant path instead. (1) Add a short 'Songs from Suno' tip to the Add music flow: 'Open your song on suno.com or in the app, then … > Download > MP3 (or WAV on Pro/Premier), then Add music'. Mention that downloads count toward Suno's monthly or trial allowance, and that only paid-plan downloads may be used commercially, for example at paid gigs. (2) Optional: add a PWA Web Share Target so Android users can share a downloaded Suno MP3/WAV from Files straight into a TwinSpin deck. It works on the static site through the service worker; iOS doesn't support it. (3) Optional and low priority: on the music-player page only, accept a suno.com/song/<id> link and show the official suno.com/embed/<id> iframe for listening, clearly labelled as playback inside Suno's player and not part of the DJ decks. (4) Watch Suno's developer API partner program, announced 2026-07-01. If it ever adds OAuth and licensed access to a user's own song files, check whether a browser PKCE flow can feed decodeAudioData. Until then, the share video, the obfuscated m4a-opus stream, proxies and cookie wrappers all break Suno's 2026-09-03 Terms, so don't use them, even though CORS would let some of them through.
## Options
### A. Download from Suno, then use Add music in TwinSpin (recommended)
- **whatWorks**: Everything. The file goes through the existing decodeAudioData path, so the full DJ engine works: BPM and beat map, scratching, two-deck mixing, EQ/FX, queue, shuffle, repeat, transitions. Optional extras: help text such as 'From Suno: open your song, then … > Download > MP3/WAV, then Add music'. Optionally a PWA Web Share Target, so on Android a downloaded file can be shared from the Files app straight into TwinSpin.
- **requirements**: A Suno account. Since 2026-09-03 downloads are capped. Free: up to 7 lifetime trial downloads (accounts made after 09-03 may get fewer, but can buy more), personal and non-commercial use only. Pro: 20 a month. Premier: 60 a month. Premier in Suno Studio: no limit. Re-downloading a song, or getting it in another format or as stems, counts as one download. Extra downloads can be bought (no published price). Nothing needed on TwinSpin's side.
- **termsAndLegal**: Fully compliant: this is Suno's own download channel. Ownership and commercial use: songs downloaded on paid plans 'remain yours to use commercially or personally'. Pro/Premier output is assigned to the user. Free/Basic output is limited to 'lawful, personal and non-commercial purposes', and trial downloads are 'not eligible for commercial use'. The Terms never mention DJing. Practical reading: DJing at home or for fun is fine on any plan. A paid gig or a monetised stream needs a paid-plan download. Remixes stay personal/non-commercial. Watermark and metadata must not be removed. Third-party venues and platforms have their own rules, and rights holders may collect revenue.
- **technical**: No CORS, OAuth or DRM involved, because the file is local. MP3 decodes everywhere. WAV is the better source for beat analysis and scratching. Works on the static GitHub Pages site and on localhost as it is. Optional share_target means adding a manifest share_target (POST multipart) and having the service worker handle that POST. That also works on a static site.
- **effort**: None to small. It works today. Help copy takes about an hour. A PWA share target takes about half a day plus testing on Android. iOS Safari does not support Web Share Target.
- sources: https://about.suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos https://suno.com/terms https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13926081 https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13926209 https://jackrighteous.com/es-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-premier-60-song-download-limit-september-3-2026
### B. Official Suno embed player (iframe) on the music-player page
- **whatWorks**: Plays only. The user pastes suno.com/song/<id>, and TwinSpin shows Suno's hosted player at suno.com/embed/<id> in an iframe. No access to the audio: no decks, no BPM/beat map, no scratching, EQ/FX, crossfading or auto-mix. Playback can't be controlled reliably from TwinSpin (no documented postMessage API), so queue and transitions would be limited to swapping iframes.
- **requirements**: The song must be public or shared on Suno. No account or keys on TwinSpin's side.
- **termsAndLegal**: The Terms don't mention embedding, but Suno serves the embed page itself, so it is an 'intentionally made available' channel. This is the lowest-risk way to play a Suno link inside TwinSpin. Anything that pulls audio out of the iframe would count as ripping.
- **technical**: Tested 2026-10-13: suno.com/embed/<id> returns 200 with no X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors header, so it can be framed from https://ranama.github.io. suno.com/song/<id> sends X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN and frame-ancestors 'none', so it cannot be framed. A cross-origin iframe's audio cannot be routed into the page's AudioContext. Works from a static site.
- **effort**: Small (a few hours): parse the song link, add an iframe card to the player page. Low value for a DJ app.
- sources: https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/how-to-embed-suno-songs https://suno.com/terms
### C. Paste a Suno link and fetch the audio directly into a deck (client-side)
- **whatWorks**: Not viable for audio, in practice or under the terms. What I saw on 2026-10-13 with a public v6 song: the old cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp3 pattern returns 403 (CloudFront). cdn2.suno.ai/<id>.mp3 returns 403. The page JSON's audio_url is https://studio-api.prod.suno.com/api/forbidden. media_urls lists an 'm4a-opus' progressive file at d2lwuy8qc234o3.cloudfront.net/1/clip/<id>.m4a. That host sends ACAO * and supports ranges, but the bytes have no MP4 boxes and use all 256 byte values in the first 64 KB, so the stream is obfuscated or encrypted and decodeAudioData will fail. The share video cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp4 (H.264 + AAC, about 7.9 MB, ACAO *, CORP cross-origin) is plain and would decode, but see the terms.
- **requirements**: No account needed for public songs. That is part of the problem: it skips the per-plan download allowance.
- **termsAndLegal**: Not allowed. The Terms (effective 2026-09-03) ban 'recording or stream ripping', 'data mining, robots, scraping', and getting content 'through any means not intentionally made available'. Output used commercially must come from a permitted Download. Undoing the m4a-opus obfuscation would also count as circumventing a protection measure. The download caps were brought in to make mass export harder, so a link loader runs straight against what Suno intends.
- **technical**: CORS is not the blocker: the stream host and the video host both allow any origin. The blockers are the forbidden audio_url, the obfuscated stream, and the terms. Scraping the song page's embedded JSON is fragile; it changed between 2024's cdn1 MP3 links and 2026's forbidden plus m4a-opus.
- **effort**: Small to code (the video route), but it breaks Suno's terms and can break at any time. Not recommended. I didn't build or ship anything.
- sources: https://suno.com/terms https://about.suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos https://huggingface.co/spaces/MySafeCode/Downloadmp3fromsuno/blob/main/app.py https://www.all-ai.de/news/news26/suno-neue-regeln
### D. Tiny proxy (e.g. Cloudflare Worker) that fetches Suno audio for TwinSpin
- **whatWorks**: Nothing beyond option C. CORS already allows any origin, so a proxy fixes nothing. It would only hide the scraping and add the risk of TwinSpin redistributing Suno audio.
- **requirements**: A server or edge function. That means TwinSpin would no longer be backend-free.
- **termsAndLegal**: Same bans as option C (scraping, ripping, unintended means), and the proxy operator now handles third-party content.
- **technical**: Unnecessary for CORS. Can't solve the encrypted stream without circumvention.
- **effort**: Small to medium. Not recommended.
- sources: https://suno.com/terms
### E. Unofficial cookie-based wrappers (e.g. gcui-art/suno-api)
- **whatWorks**: In theory, generating songs and listing or getting clips with the user's session cookie. Audio fetching hits the same forbidden or obfuscated stream as option C.
- **requirements**: Node server with Playwright, the user's suno.com session Cookie header in an env var, and a paid 2Captcha key to solve the hCaptcha challenges Suno raises 'constantly' (per the README). Last commit 2026-03-06, not archived, about 3.26k stars, 108 open issues. Describes itself as 'unofficial … for learning and research purposes only'.
- **termsAndLegal**: Breaks the bans on bots and scraping, and solving CAPTCHAs automatically is bot-detection bypass. Handling users' session cookies is also a security and privacy risk. Accounts can be suspended.
- **technical**: Needs a backend, so it can't run from GitHub Pages. Cookie auth is not OAuth, so there is no PKCE or consent flow. Fragile: it breaks whenever Suno changes internal endpoints.
- **effort**: Medium to large, with ongoing upkeep. Not recommended.
- sources: https://github.com/gcui-art/suno-api https://api.github.com/repos/gcui-art/suno-api https://suno.com/terms
### F. Official Suno developer API (future)
- **whatWorks**: Not available as of 2026-10-13. Announced 2026-07-01 in a LinkedIn post by CPO Jack Brody as 'exploring a developer API', starting with 'a curated group of partners'. The stated focus is new generative-music experiences, meaning generation, not library playback. Nothing published about library access, download endpoints or audio delivery.
- **requirements**: Apply through the intake form linked from Brody's post. Selection is curated. No docs, pricing, quotas or launch date.
- **termsAndLegal**: Unknown. Partner terms would decide whether DJ or playback use is allowed.
- **technical**: Unknown. If it ever offers OAuth plus licensed downloads of a user's own songs, a static-site PKCE flow could become possible. Nothing confirms this.
- **effort**: None now. Possibly worth applying if TwinSpin wants a 'generate a transition or track with Suno' feature.
- sources: https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/07/03/suno-is-opening-an-api-partner-program/ https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-explores-developer-api-seeking-apps-that-unlock-experiences-generative-music-makes-possible-for-the-first-time https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-tools-news/suno-developer-api/
## Findings
- [high,critical] No official public or self-serve Suno API exists as of 2026-10-13. On 2026-07-01 Suno's CPO said it is 'exploring a developer API' with a curated partner group through an intake form. No docs, pricing or launch date, and the focus is generative experiences, not library playback.
- [high,critical] Tested 2026-10-13 on public song f0f00357-0cd7-4fd5-96e5-d9314439f4e5 (v6, created 2026-09-09, 240.4 s). The page's audio_url is 'https://studio-api.prod.suno.com/api/forbidden'. cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp3 and cdn2.suno.ai/<id>.mp3 both return 403. So the old 'cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp3' trick no longer works, at least for current songs.
- [medium,critical] Suno's player now streams an 'm4a-opus' file (encoding 1.0.0) from d2lwuy8qc234o3.cloudfront.net/1/clip/<id>.m4a. It sends Access-Control-Allow-Origin: * and allows range requests, but the payload has no MP4 boxes (ftyp/moov/mdat) and full byte entropy, so it looks obfuscated or encrypted. Web Audio's decodeAudioData cannot use it directly, and undoing the protection would be circumvention.
- [high] The public share video cdn1.suno.ai/<id>.mp4 is a plain MP4 (H.264 + AAC, about 7.9 MB) with ACAO * and CORP cross-origin. Technically decodable cross-origin, but pulling audio from it skips the download allowance and conflicts with the Terms.
- [high] CORS is not the limiting factor for Suno media: the audio stream, video and splash-clip hosts all send ACAO *. A proxy would add nothing legitimate. The blockers are the forbidden audio_url, the obfuscated stream, and the Terms.
- [high,critical] Suno Terms (last revised 2026-08-10, effective 2026-09-03) ban 'data mining, robots, scraping', say 'recording or stream ripping are prohibited', and bar access 'through any means not intentionally made available'. They also allow commercial use of Output only if it is a permitted Download under the user's tier.
- [high,critical] Download caps since 2026-09-03 (announced 2026-08-10, following the Nov 2025 Warner settlement that said downloads would need paid accounts). Free: up to 7 lifetime trial downloads, non-commercial (accounts made after 09-03 may get fewer, more can be bought). Pro: 20 a month. Premier: 60 a month. Premier in Suno Studio: unlimited. The caps also apply to older songs. Every song stays playable and shareable on Suno on every plan.
- [high] Download formats: MP3 on all plans (web and mobile), WAV for Pro/Premier on suno.com, video, stems where they exist (no extra charge), and MIDI for Studio songs on Premier. One song counts as one download whatever the format, and re-downloads are free.
- [high] Ownership and commercial use: Pro/Premier output is assigned to the user, and paid-plan downloads 'remain yours to use commercially or personally'. Free/Basic output is personal and non-commercial only, and trial downloads are not eligible for commercial use. Remixes are personal and non-commercial only. DJ use is not addressed specifically; paid gigs or monetised streams need a paid-plan download.
- [high] gcui-art/suno-api is unofficial ('for learning and research purposes only'). It needs the user's suno.com session cookie plus paid 2Captcha solving of hCaptcha via Playwright on a server. Last commit 2026-03-06, not archived. It goes against the Terms' bot and scraping bans and cannot run from a static site.
- [high] The official embed player suno.com/embed/<id> returns 200 with no X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors, so it can be embedded from ranama.github.io. The suno.com/song/<id> page itself cannot be framed (frame-ancestors 'none'). An embed's audio cannot reach TwinSpin's Web Audio graph.
- [high,critical] No OAuth or PKCE sign-in exists for third-party apps. Suno account access for other apps exists only through cookie copying in unofficial tools, so a static-site 'connect your Suno account' button is not possible today.
