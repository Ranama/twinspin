TwinSpin

Privacy

TwinSpin is a DJ app and music player that runs in your browser. It has no server of its own, no account, no ads and no tracking. Nothing leaves your device except the requests to the services you connect yourself.

In short: your songs, set list and settings stay on this device. TwinSpin only talks to Google Drive or your music server when you use them, and to the second device you link for headphones.

What stays on your device

  • Your songs (the files you add, and copies you save offline) and their details (title, artist, BPM, length), in your browser's storage for this site.
  • Your set list, settings and outputs, in the same storage.
  • A music server sign-in key (never your password), with the server's address and your user name, only when you tick “Remember on this device”. Otherwise it is kept only until you close the app.
  • Google Drive access lasts about an hour and is kept only in memory: it is gone when you close the app. TwinSpin only notes that you allowed it once, so Google does not ask again. TwinSpin never gets your Google password.
  • Saved Google Drive links: the Drive links you import (the link, the folder or song name and how many songs it had), so you can import them again with one tap. They are stored only on this device and never sent anywhere; Google only sees a link when you import it.

Remove everything at any time with Songs > Clear library, Settings > Music server > Disconnect, Settings > Google Drive > Sign out, Add music > Paste a Google Drive link > Saved links (Remove, or Forget all saved links), Settings > Reset, or your browser's “clear site data” for this site.

What is sent, and only when you use it

  • Google Drive (if you use it): Google's sign-in and picker run on Google's own pages and scripts, loaded only when you open Add music. TwinSpin asks only for access to the files you pick (the drive.file permission). It downloads those songs straight to this device. It cannot see the rest of your Drive. Google's privacy policy applies to your Google account. You can remove TwinSpin's access in your Google Account > Security > Third-party apps, or with Settings > Sign out of Google Drive.
  • Google Drive links you paste: the files behind the link are fetched from Google with this app's public API key. No sign-in.
  • Your music server (Navidrome or another Subsonic server, for example on your NAS): TwinSpin connects to the address you type, with your user name and a key made from your password. Song lists, cover art and songs come straight from your server to this device.
  • Second device (headphone mix over Wi-Fi): the two devices connect directly. A public signalling service (PeerJS) helps them find each other, and its code and the QR code maker load from a public code server (CDN) only when you use it; the audio goes between your devices.
  • Fonts: the app's fonts come from Google Fonts.

Requests to other sites carry only TwinSpin's site address as the referrer (never the page or your songs).

What TwinSpin never does

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No uploads: your songs are never sent anywhere by TwinSpin.
  • No passwords stored.

Contact

TwinSpin is open source. Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/Ranama/twinspin.
